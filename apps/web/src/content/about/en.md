# What SOTF Mods is

SOTF Mods is the community home of **Sons of the Forest** modding. Since 2023 it has hosted mods, libraries and BuildShare builds for **RedLoader**, the game's mod loader, and it is the largest catalogue of RedLoader mods there is.

Everything is free to download, straight from our storage: no account, no waiting timer and no download manager. RedManager, the community's mod manager, reads the same catalogue, so you can install any mod from here in one click.

# Who runs it

The site is run by a small volunteer team of players and modders, and moderated by **moderators**: long-standing community members who check new uploads and handle reports. The code of the site is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/ChoqueCastroLD/sotf-mods-api).

Creators own their mods. We host them, show them and help people find them; the credit and the decisions about each mod belong to its author.

# How we keep downloads trustworthy

- Every new mod is reviewed by a moderator before it goes public.
- Every file is scanned, and each version shows its size and SHA-256 checksum so you can verify what you downloaded.
- Anyone can report a mod, a comment or a review; moderators act on reports quickly and log every action.
- Adult content is hidden unless you opt in.

Read the [content policy](/content-policy) for what is allowed.

# How the site is paid for

Hosting and storage cost money. Guests see a few ads, never next to a download button and never on install steps; signed-in members see none. We don't sell personal data, and our own statistics work without cookies. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

# Contact

The fastest way to reach us is the community [Discord](https://discord.gg/sotf). For privacy requests, copyright notices or security issues, use the contacts listed on the [privacy](/privacy) and [DMCA](/dmca) pages.
