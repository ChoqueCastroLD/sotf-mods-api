Quality-of-life mods don't change the game's balance; they remove friction. Bigger stacks, faster crafting, cleaner menus and fewer trips back to base are the usual first installs.

Most of them are client-side, so you can add them to a co-op session without anyone else installing them. Check the multiplayer label on each card, and the compatibility badge after every game patch.
