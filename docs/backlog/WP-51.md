## Decisions to confirm

- **Post-review "done"** · the audit log is the state: `version.post_review_approve` (approve on an active version) takes it out of the lane; unreviewed versions leave the lane after 30 days.
- **Report hide semantics** · mod → `unlisted`, version → held (`pending`), kit → `unlisted`, comment/review/compat report → `hidden`; users are never hidden. Dismissing restores only what reports hid (latest audit entry of the target is `report.auto_hide|report.hide_target`).
- **Resolving closes siblings** · resolving/dismissing one report closes every open report of the same target; each reporter gets `report.resolved`.
- **Own content** · reporting your own content is a 409 (authors use "request removal").
- **Sanctions** · `suspend` requires `endsAt`; `ban` is permanent (no `endsAt`) and revokes every session; admins cannot change other admins (use `pnpm admin:grant`).
- **Announcement signal** · `announcement.published` (signal to everyone) only when the window has already started at creation.
- **Builds are not scanned** by VirusTotal (JSON blueprints); the quota is kept for zips.
- **VirusTotal quota ledger** · `AnalyticsEvent` rows of kind `virustotal_call` (≤ 500/day, 90-day retention by `cleanup.analytics`), counted under an advisory lock.
