## Decisions taken (confirm or adjust)

- **Notification emails bypass `email.send`** · `packages/core/src/notifications/email.ts` · the WP-30 outbox job only knows `EMAIL_TEMPLATE_PAYLOADS` (WP-30 path), so notification rows (`template = 'notify.*'`) are written to the same "EmailOutbox" and delivered by the notification jobs (`deliverNotificationEmail`: same claim/retry/idempotency semantics, retried by every `10m` digest run). If a single delivery path is wanted, WP-30 adds `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_PAYLOADS` to `EMAIL_TEMPLATE_PAYLOADS` and `renderNotificationEmail` (`@sotf/emails/notifications/index`) to `templateRenderer()`; then the digests can call `queueEmail` instead.
- **Instant = 30-second flush** · `packages/core/src/notifications/service.ts` · instant signals schedule `notifications.digest {frequency: '10m'}` with a deterministic job id per 30-second window (one email per user per window); `comment.on_my_mod` waits for the 10-minute schedule ("lotes de 10 min").
- **Gamification signals in the weekly report** · `packages/core/src/notifications/creator-weekly.ts` · §7.3 says milestones, badges and awards have no email but "enter the weekly summary": they are listed as highlights of `creator.weekly_report` (which has its own opt-out), not added to the weekly digest.
- **Legacy mention drain trigger** · `apps/worker/src/jobs/digests/index.ts` · `JOB_SCHEDULES` has no `legacy.mentions` entry, so every `10m` digest run enqueues it (one job per 10-minute window) when `LEGACY_COEXIST=false`. Optional: add `{ queue: 'legacy.mentions', cron: '*/10 * * * *', key: 'every-10m', data: {} }` to `JOB_SCHEDULES` (`packages/contracts/src/jobs.ts`, WP-11) and drop the trigger.
- **Signal actor tiers** · `packages/core/src/notifications/queries.ts` · `NotificationDTO.actor.creatorTier`/`survivorRank` are `null` until gamification (WP-60) exposes a shared `UserRefDTO` builder; switch to it then.
