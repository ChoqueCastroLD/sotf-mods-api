Only works with keyboard & mouse

Description
In-game paper map you can write on. Marks already-explored areas as you walk. Optionally hides your position. Toolbar to select different modes. Protractors help you triangulate your position. Has a few configurable options.

Highly Recommended Complementary Mods
I highly recommend you combine this mod with these other mods I made to bring back the original "The Forest" experience:
GPS Tracker Disabled or Compass
Remove KnightVs and Golf Carts

Installation instructions

  • Install RedLoader if you haven't already.
  • Unzip to your game's main folder (where SonsOfTheForest.exe is located) maintaining the ZIP's folder structure.
    (The .DLL will end up under the Mods folder, and the .json under the Mods/RW18_PaperMap folder.) Use
  • Bring it up by pressing the "N" key.
  • Zoom: Zoom in/out using the mouse wheel or the middle mouse button.
  • Pan: Right-click and drag to pan the map.
  • Toolbar at the top: Select between pan, write, erase, protractor 1 and protractor 2 modes. Instructions for each mode are shown on the bottom-left. Configurable Options
  • Enable player marker (WARNING!!!: To re-enable the marker, you'll be asked to click on your current position on the map. This is so that you're not tempted to re-enable it to peek your current position if you're lost)
  • Reveal radius: How much is "revealed" as you walk.
  • Must click current position to reveal map: Click on your position on the map to mark areas recently explored. This is so that if you're lost, you can't use recently-revealed areas to hint at your position.
  • Reveal terrain while flying: Mark overflown areas as explored in the map.
