Features:

  • Hold leftAlt (can be changed in the settings) when placing a structure from the guide book to ignore restrictions.
  • This feature can also be used when you're trying to place objects on ground like Logs, BoneFires, Standing Sticks , Tarp, Solar Panels etc..
  • Press CapsLock (can be changed in the settings) when placing a structure to toggle Manual Placement Mode.
  • The Manual Placement Mode will give you more control over where you place your structures by disabling Auto snap to the ground and you can scroll the Mouse Wheel to adjust the placement distance from the player.
  • You can adjust the mouse wheel scrolling sensitivity in the settings to change how much scrolling it will affect the placement distance.
  • Press Tab (can be changed in the settings) to change the rotation axis in the manual placement mode (with the exception of platforms with foundations).
  • You can show/hide the rotation axes in the settings, and customize their size.
