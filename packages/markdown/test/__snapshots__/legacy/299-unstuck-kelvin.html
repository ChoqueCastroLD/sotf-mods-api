Description
Kelvin keeps getting stuck. Press K to teleport him in front of you.

Installation instructions

  • Install RedLoader if you haven't already.
  • Unzip to your game's main folder (where SonsOfTheForest.exe is located) maintaining the ZIP's folder structure.
    (The .DLL will end up under the Mods folder, and the .json under the Mods/RW18_unstuckKelvin folder.) Main features
    Press K to teleport Kelvin in front of you when he gets stuck. Requirements
    Must have RedLoader installed.
