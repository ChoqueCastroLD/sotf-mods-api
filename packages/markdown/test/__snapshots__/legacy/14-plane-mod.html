How to spawn?#

Enable game debug console (type "cheatstick" while playing and press F1) than do "spawnplane" command

Controls#

Interact button to enter, G to exit
W & S for throttle
A & D for yaw
Mouse for pitch & roll
Hold RMB to unlock camera movement

Interact button to enter, circle to exit
Right & Left triggers for throttle
L1 & R1 for yaw
Left stick for pitch & roll
Right stick to rotate camera

Settings#

Additional settings for camera adjustment and pitch/roll direction in the Mods panel (Pause menu -> mods)

Note#

Plane position will be saved and shared globally between savegames
You can spawn a new one at any time
Nicks Mod Menu and/or NoClip mods seems to break this mod causing the player to slide on the ground
