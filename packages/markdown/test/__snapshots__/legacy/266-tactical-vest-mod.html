This mod adds lots of options related to cosmetic tactical gear, for both you and for co-op players. It's client-side meaning only you will see any changes on you or on others, but your friends can download the mod to see it too :)

Check the mod settings to configure it to your taste. Settings are applied in certain specific circumstances, so expect to reload/rejoin to fully notice changes.

It even works with the
game's outfits! (Helmet mileage may vary, expect it to clip with armor if unhidden)

Full options list:

  • Tactical vest
  • Helmet (dead corpse “hemlet” model by default)
  • FAST/Cutscene helmet model swap (requires helmet to be enabled)
  • Name tags for other players (displays Steam usernames, works best if you disable visible world icons in SOTF’s settings)
  • Hide backpacks (for a cleaner look)
  • Gloves (for first-person and co-op players)
  • "Old jacket" option, like Kelvin and cutscene guys wear. This is the only clothing piece that was designed for the gloves.
  • Balaclavas and/or sunglasses (to look like the intro pilot)
  • Hide equipped armor pieces (such as creepy or bone armor)
  • Option to give Kelvin his helmet back

    The binoculars break the first-person gloves, but changing outfits fixes them.
