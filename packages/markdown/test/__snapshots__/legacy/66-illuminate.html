ILLUMINATE#

FEATURES#

  • Change the rendering distance of various lights (light bulbs, torches, spotlights).
  • Change the intensity of various lights (lighter, flashlight, lightbulbs, torches, spotlights)
  • Change the color of lights (lighter, flashlight, torches)
  • Change the color of each individual light bulb (gets saved into your save game)

LIGHT BULB COLOR#

To change the color of the light bulbs go into the settings of the mod, scroll to the bottom and under ColorSwatch add the color strings of the colors you want to cycle through (You can use the google color picker for example to copy the strings https://g.co/kgs/BfM2vm). Then go up to a light bulb, switch it on and cycle through the colors using your mouse wheel.
