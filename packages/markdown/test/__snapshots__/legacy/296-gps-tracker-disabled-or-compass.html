Description
Disables the GPS tracker either immediately or after a random amount of time. Once disabled, it can optionally turn into a compass. The maximum potential time that it will work, and whether it will turn into a compass are configurable options.

Highly Recommended Complementary Mods
This mod is meant to be combined with my other mod:
Writable Paper Map

Installation instructions

  • Install RedLoader if you haven't already.
  • Unzip to your game's main folder (where SonsOfTheForest.exe is located) maintaining the ZIP's folder structure.
    (The .DLL will end up under the Mods folder, and the .json under the Mods/RW18_GPSdown folder.) Main features
  • Disables the GPS tracker, either immediately or after a random amount of time.
  • Once disabled, it can turn into a compass or show a "NO SIGNAL" message depending on your settings.
  • The maximum potential time that it will work is configurable. It will randomly stop working at some point before your set limit.
  • Even in "NO SIGNAL" mode the tracker is not completely useless - it still emits beeps for nearby GPS tags.
    You can actually get lost in the forest. Waking up in a random village without knowing where you are hits different now!
